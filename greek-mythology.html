Where these stories come from

Greek mythology wasn't written once — it grew over centuries, through poets like Homer and Hesiod, through vase painters, playwrights, and travelling storytellers who each shaped the gods a little differently. The versions here are the most commonly told, but you'll find variations everywhere you look, which is part of what kept them alive for three thousand years.
